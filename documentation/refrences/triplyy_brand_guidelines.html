Logo rules
Do
  • Always keep the trailing dot — it's part of the mark
  • Use Syne 800 weight only for the wordmark
  • Minimum size: 120px width on digital
  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "T" on all sides
  • Use approved color variants only
Don't
  • Remove the dot or replace it with another character
  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the wordmark
  • Use the logo smaller than 80px wide
  • Place the logo on busy backgrounds
  • Recreate the logo in a different font

05Voice and tone
Direct
Say the thing. No fluff, no filler. Backpackers are pragmatic people who hate wasted words.
"8 hostel deals for Istanbul. Under $30/night."
Not: "We've curated a wonderful selection..."
Confident
We know the deals are good. No hedging, no maybe. State things plainly and stand by them.
"This is the best hostel in Taksim for solo travelers."
Not: "This might be a good option for..."
Warm, not hype
We're a travel-savvy friend, not a salesperson. Excited about the deal but not screaming about it.
"Free breakfast and a rooftop. Hard to beat at $19."
Not: "AMAZING DEAL!!! Don't miss out!!!"
Specific
Vague is useless. Dates, prices, distances — always. A deal without a number isn't a deal.
"200m from Hagia Sophia. $19/night. 4.7 stars."
Not: "Conveniently located near top sights."

Principle: motion should inform, not perform. Animations exist to show state change or guide attention — not to look impressive. Always respect prefers-reduced-motion.
